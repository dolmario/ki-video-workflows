# Resistors: original companion worksheets

English edition. The film is technically complete; full listening review and practical validation remain open. Solutions are theoretical calculations, not measurements collected by us. Eight proposed activities total 210 minutes of practical learning; timing has not been empirically validated. The instructor must check the actual components, datasheets, supply, meter and wiring before activities. No mains-side work.

Open WORKSHEETS.html in a browser and print it. Read VERSUCHE.md first; actual measurement cells are blank. Consult LOESUNGEN.md after your own calculations. DIAGRAMME contains original labelled SVG diagrams. The isolated low-voltage proposals do not replace actual practical setup approval.
